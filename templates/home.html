Postman Endpoints

POST
/api/auth/register/

Email registration. Sends OTP and waits for verification.

POST
/api/auth/register/unified/

Email, phone, or both. Sends OTP to configured channels.

POST
/api/auth/register/verify/

Verify email or phone OTP, then receive JWT tokens.

POST
/api/auth/login/

Login after email or phone verification.

POST
/api/auth/otp/resend/

Resend registration or forgot-password OTP.
